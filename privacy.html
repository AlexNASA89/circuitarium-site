Circuitarium (circuitarium.com) publishes short educational 3D videos about how computer chips work, on YouTube and Instagram.

Website

This website is a static page. It does not use accounts, forms, cookies or analytics, and it does not collect personal information.

Circuitarium Uploader

"Circuitarium Uploader" is an internal tool used only by the Circuitarium team to upload and schedule videos on our own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It accesses only our own channel. It does not access, collect, store or share data of any other Google user.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
